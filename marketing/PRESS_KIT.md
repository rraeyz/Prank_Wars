# Prank Wars — Basın kiti / Press kit

**Developer:** idris (solo developer, Erzurum, Türkiye)
**Platform:** Windows (Steam). Steam Deck later.
**Price:** $4.99 (regional pricing on)
**Players:** 2–8 online (1v1 to 4v4), practice & sandbox with bots
**Genre:** party / physics comedy

## One line
Booby-trap the house, then watch your friends walk right into it.

## Features
- Two teams take turns: one rigs the house in 60 seconds, the other has 100 seconds to do the chores.
- 14 pranks: banana peels, buckets over doors, paint bombs in fridges, flour sacks, giant springs, marbles, a rogue robot vacuum...
- Chain reactions double your points with every extra link.
- Slow-motion replay of the best prank every round — made for clips.
- Sandbox mode to build ridiculous chains for videos.
- No blood, no violence: family- and stream-friendly.

## Assets
- Screenshots: run `godot --path . --resolution 1920x1080 -- --keyart=<folder>` on a PC with a GPU, or press **F9** in game (saved to the game's user folder).
- Logo text: "PRANK WARS" in yellow (#FFE14D) with dark outline.
