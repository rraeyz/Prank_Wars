# Mağaza metinleri (rapordan, oyunun güncel hâline göre)

## Short description (EN)
Booby-trap the house. Watch your friends walk right into it. Prank Wars is a chaotic 2–8 player physics comedy where two teams take turns rigging a home with pranks — and the best chain reaction wins.

## Kısa açıklama (TR)
Evi şakalarla donat, arkadaşlarının tam içine yürümesini izle. Prank Wars, iki takımın sırayla evi tuzakladığı 2–8 kişilik kaotik bir fizik komedisi. En iyi zincirleme şakayı kuran kazanır.

## Long description (EN)
**Set it up. Watch it go wrong. Laugh until you can't breathe.**

Two teams. One house. Ninety seconds to hide banana peels, buckets over doors, flour sacks and paint-loaded fridges. Then the other team has to do their chores — make a sandwich, take a shower, find the remote — while everything you built goes off.

- **14 pranks** you can place anywhere — some go off by themselves, some you fire at the perfect moment
- **Chain reactions:** one slip knocks over the next trap — every extra link doubles your score
- **Wobbly bean physics** that make every fall a highlight
- **Instant replays:** the best prank of each round, in slow motion
- **1v1 up to 4v4** online with friends, or practice against bots
- No blood, no violence — just a very, very messy house
