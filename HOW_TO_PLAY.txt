PRANK WARS - How to play / Nasil oynanir
========================================

EN
- Two teams take turns. TRAPPERS rig the house (90 s), RUNNERS then do 3 chores (150 s).
- Trapper: Q/R or mouse wheel = pick prank, Left click / F = place. Budget: 15 per round.
- During chores the trappers watch: Tab = change camera, WASD = pan, 1-9 = fire remote pranks.
- Runner: WASD move, Space jump, Shift dive, hold E = do chore / open / disarm / grab, Left click = throw.
- After 20 s of chores, watching trappers become GHOSTS: G / right click flings a nearby object at a runner.
- One prank knocking into another = CHAIN. Every extra link doubles the points (10, 20, 40, 80...).
- Esc = menu. Gamepad works too (A jump, X interact, B dive, RB action, LB/RB-dpad pick prank, Y camera).
- Play with friends: one player clicks "Host Game", the others "Join" with the host's IP
  (same Wi-Fi: local IP; over the internet: port 24680 UDP forwarding, or a free virtual LAN
  like Radmin VPN / ZeroTier / Tailscale). If Steam is running, "Host on Steam" lets you invite friends.

TR
- Iki takim sirayla oynar. KURANLAR evi tuzaklar (90 sn), KOSUCULAR sonra 3 gorev yapar (150 sn).
- Kuran: Q/R veya fare tekerlegi = saka sec, Sol tik / F = yerlestir. Butce: raunt basina 15.
- Gorev sirasinda kuranlar izler: Tab = kamera, WASD = kaydir, 1-9 = uzaktan saka tetikle.
- Kosucu: WASD yuru, Space zipla, Shift dalis, E basili tut = gorev / ac / etkisiz birak / al, Sol tik = firlat.
- Gorevlerin 20. saniyesinden sonra izleyen kuranlar HAYALET olur: G / sag tik yakindaki esyayi kosucuya firlatir.
- Bir saka digerini tetiklerse ZINCIR olur. Her ek halka puani ikiye katlar (10, 20, 40, 80...).
- Esc = menu. Gamepad de calisir.
- Arkadaslarla: biri "Oyun Kur", digerleri kurucunun IP adresiyle "Katil".
  (Ayni Wi-Fi: yerel IP; internetten: 24680 UDP port yonlendirme ya da ucretsiz sanal LAN:
  Radmin VPN / ZeroTier / Tailscale). Steam aciksa "Steam ile Kur" ile arkadas davet edebilirsin.
